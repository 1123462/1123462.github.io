Coursework notes

This page introduces three parts of my coursework: video streaming with sockets, word counting with MapReduce, and classification with Spark.

What the coursework covers

TCP and UDP video streaming

The same video is sent over TCP and UDP. The experiment records received frames, completion times and packet traces to compare delivery under the tested conditions.

Python · Sockets · OpenCV · Wireshark

Word counting with MapReduce

A Python implementation separates mapping, shuffling and reducing. Hadoop and Spark then process the same text, with word counts and local execution times compared.

Python · Hadoop · Spark

Wine classification with Spark

A Spark MLlib pipeline prepares the wine dataset and trains a logistic regression model. Model selection uses training data; held-out predictions are used for the final evaluation.

PySpark · MLlib
